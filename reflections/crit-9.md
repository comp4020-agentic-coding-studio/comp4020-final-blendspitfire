# Crit 9 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The biggest breakthrough was probably on the art side. But I find it hard to
call it a real "breakthrough", because it didn't meet what I'd hoped for at
the start; it was more of a compromise: out of the many art styles I was
reasonably interested in, I picked one the AI happens to be good at.

The other was finally settling the gameplay. I wouldn't say the current
gameplay is excellent, or anything that makes you sit up, but it feels
self-consistent to me, and for my own abilities and what this assignment
asks for, I think that's plenty.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

In the way I'm developing now, art is a much harder part than gameplay.
That's not to say designing fun gameplay is easy, but it's easy to get the
AI aligned on, and easy to describe. If I change my mind, then as long as
the project isn't too big yet, tearing the whole game down and rebuilding it
is a one-sentence request. Visuals are different: at this stage, they need a
person to keep checking, reviewing and revising them. I don't think that's
necessarily a bad thing. Games are, at least in part, a medium for art, and
visuals really are an important part of personal expression. The process
also helped me get much clearer about what I actually want to see.
