# Recorded sounds

All CC0 (public domain); credited anyway. Source: The Free Firearm Sound
Library, via [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library).
Each file is one shot, near-mic, cut to 0.4-0.5 s from just before the
peak, mixed to mono 48 kHz, its outdoor tail faded (the game's room
reverb supplies the space).
